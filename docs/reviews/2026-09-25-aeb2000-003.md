### SEC-16-003: The history scan's term list misses the employer's own name and email domain, and may be case-sensitive

- Severity: LOW
- Disposition: RECOMMENDATION
- Category: privacy
- Where: `.review/aeb2000/history-scan.txt:211`: the terms are `employer|/Users/|Argus|Hetland|Thor Henning|NODE-M22|Zscaler`
- Status: new

The scan looks for the word "employer", but not for the employer's actual name or its email domain. A sentence in an earlier commit that named the employer directly, without the word "employer", would not show up. The pattern also looks case-sensitive, so a lowercase `argus` (in a path, say) would slip past it. I checked HEAD myself with a case-insensitive search, including the employer's name and domain, the owner's first name and private address ranges, and found nothing. I cannot check earlier commits the same way.

The report deliberately does not name the employer.

**Fix:** Before pushing, run the same scan over the range once more, case-insensitive (`git log -p -i -G ...` or `grep -i`), adding the employer's name and email domain. Leave `drargus` excluded. If it finds nothing, push. When SEC-14 is taken into the script, take the term list from a local file that is not tracked, so the sensitive terms themselves are never committed.
