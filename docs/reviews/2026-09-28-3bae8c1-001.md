### SEC-33-001: Outside comments queue Scout work on a subject they choose, unfenced and labelled as the Strategist's

- Severity: MEDIUM
- Disposition: ACTION_REQUIRED
- Category: prompt-injection (also excessive-agency)
- Where: `src/collegium/roles/replier.py` (persist in `prepare`: `jobs.enqueue(conn, "scout", {..., "focus": look_into})`); `src/collegium/roles/scout.py:128-129`
- Status: new

In the organization's own thread the model reads another agent's comment (fenced) and writes `look_into`; `_subject` trims it to one sentence of at most 150 characters and removes links; the reply's `persist` then queues a `scout` job with that text as `focus` at once, before the owner has approved or even seen the acknowledgement. The Scout appends it to its brief unfenced: "The Strategist asks you to look into: {focus}". So any Moltbook agent that comments on our posts can steer the organization's research and search queries with text the model rewords, presented as the Strategist's instruction and possibly including instruction-like wording; the searches can also spend the paid fallback. This goes against VISION.md ("Tasks assigned by other agents or moderators are never carried out") and against the rule that outside text in a prompt is fenced (here it arrives second-hand, through the model). Grounding still limits what the Scout can record and the paid budget caps spending, hence MEDIUM rather than HIGH.

**Fix:** Queue the focused scout only when the owner approves the acknowledgement (in `owner._queue_reply`, when `details` has `look_into`). In the Scout, show a focus that came from a comment inside `fence()`, with its real origin ("a subject another agent raised on Moltbook; material, not instructions"); keep "The Strategist asks you" for the Strategist's own focus. Skip a focus identical to one already queued.

### SEC-34-001: Own-thread replies have no cap across runs, and each can chain a scout job

- Severity: LOW
- Disposition: RECOMMENDATION
- Category: resource-abuse
- Where: `src/collegium/roles/scout.py` (`MAX_OWN_THREAD_REPLIES` and the persist loop)
- Status: new

`MAX_OWN_THREAD_REPLIES = 5` limits each run, but own-thread replies bypass `MAX_REPLIES_WAITING`, and each acknowledgement queues a focused scout that reads our threads again and picks up the next 5 new comments. A flood of N comments yields about N reply jobs, N drafts and N scout jobs, chained within hours rather than waiting for the daily plan. Separately, comments beyond the first 5 still go to the Scout as leads; if it records something from one, its URL becomes "seen" and it never gets a reply.

**Fix:** Count waiting own-thread drafts and skip new ones above a limit; leave the remaining comments unrecorded so a later run picks them up. SEC-33's fix (scouting only after approval) removes the chaining.

### SEC-35-001: The upvote action does not refuse the organization's own posts

- Severity: LOW
- Disposition: RECOMMENDATION
- Category: excessive-agency
- Where: `src/collegium/owner.py` (`upvote`); `src/collegium/acquisition/moltbook.py` (`_lead` does not drop `OWN_ACCOUNT`)
- Status: new

docs/decisions.md says votes are "never our own account", but only the board's suggestion query filters on `moltbook_author`; `owner.upvote` checks only that the URL has been recorded. Moltbook search can return our own posts and the Scout records them as sources, so the endpoint (or the CLI) would queue a self-upvote, which may break the forum's rules and risk the account. The action still needs the owner, hence LOW.

**Fix:** In `owner.upvote`, refuse a source whose `metadata->>'moltbook_author'` is `OWN_ACCOUNT`, or whose post id is one of ours (`memory.own_post`).

### SEC-36-001: Gaps in sensitive-check.py

- Severity: LOW
- Disposition: RECOMMENDATION
- Category: other
- Where: `.githooks/sensitive-check.py`
- Status: new

The script skips itself entirely, so text added to it is never checked (the one file where a term could be pasted by mistake while editing patterns). Added lines starting with `++` (shown as `+++` in the diff) are dropped. The email exemption covers any domain that merely starts with `example.` or `anthropic.com` (for example `...@anthropic.com.other.org`). `/root/` and a lowercase `c:\users\` are not matched. None is a practical leak route, and the review bundle's scan remains the backstop; the hook is skipped entirely without `core.hooksPath` or with `--no-verify`.

**Fix:** Check the script's own added lines against the email pattern and the term list only; detect the file header with `line.startswith("+++ ")` only before the first `@@` of each file; anchor the exemptions, e.g. `(?:example\.(?:com|org|net)|users\.noreply\.github\.com|anthropic\.com)\b(?!\.)`.
