### SEC-47-001: The board's exposure surface widens with `COLLEGIUM_WEB_BIND_ADDR`, by owner's explicit, documented choice

- Severity: INFO
- Disposition: INFORMATIONAL
- Category: config
- Where: `compose.yaml:168-176`, `.env.example` (new block), `docs/decisions.md` ("The board, reachable over the owner's own network")
- Status: new

The board has no login yet; until now its only protection was listening on `127.0.0.1` alone. This change lets it listen on a private-network address instead, reachable from every device on the owner's Tailscale tailnet. `docs/decisions.md` records the tradeoff explicitly (rejected `0.0.0.0`, weighed the larger LAN exposure against it, accepted the private-overlay exposure as "materially smaller", noted the missing `__Host-` cookie prefix since the board is plain HTTP). This is exactly the kind of decision the owner is entitled to make, and it is well-reasoned and on the record, so it does not block the push; recorded here for completeness since it is a genuine increase in attack surface (anything on the tailnet, not just the owner's own devices sitting at the keyboard, can now reach an unauthenticated interface that approves Moltbook posts and database-affecting actions).

Nothing in the app can enforce "never `0.0.0.0`" — the container's Uvicorn always binds `0.0.0.0` inside its own network namespace; only Docker's port-publish address (outside the container) controls external reach — so the warning comment in `compose.yaml` and `.env.example` is the only defense against an operator mistake. Acceptable as documented.

**Fix:** none required; keep as is. If the owner later wants defense in depth here, the cheapest step would be a login (already an acknowledged gap), not a code change to this mechanism.
